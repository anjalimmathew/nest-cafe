*In collaboration with solidcore instructors*

Just like exercise strengthens your body, civic engagement strengthens your community.

We teamed up with solidcore instructors to host a free Pilates class for Rice students in the main academic quad, and because it was peak voting season, we set up our table with easy, accessible, nonpartisan resources to help students get registered and informed. A play on words we couldn't resist: exercising your right.

The policies that shape healthcare, education, women's health access, and food systems touch our everyday lives. We wanted those conversations to sit right beside a wellness space, because they belong there just as much as anything else.

### Mark your calendar

Primary runoff elections: May 26, 2026 (for races where no candidate hit 50% on March 3). General election: November 3, 2026. If you're not registered yet, do it now, so you'll be ready when it counts.
