From a dream to reality. Our doorway full of shoes, everyone gathered around the island, trusting us enough to try something we made by hand.

Our very first pop-up was about the simplest version of what Nest is: friends in one room, real food, and a reason to slow down together. We laid everything out and pinned up little notes on what each thing on the menu quietly does for your body. Not to make food clinical, just to celebrate that everyday ingredients care for you.

## What's inside your plate?

<div class="fact-grid">
<div class="fact-row"><p class="fact-row__name">Lemon Blueberry Muffin</p><p>Blueberries are one of the most antioxidant-dense fruits, rich in anthocyanins that support brain health and memory. Lemon adds vitamin C, which helps your body absorb iron from other foods through the day.</p></div>
<div class="fact-row"><p class="fact-row__name">Sea Salt Chocolate Chip Cookie</p><p>Dark chocolate contains flavonoids that improve blood flow and may support focus. A small amount of salt helps maintain electrolyte balance and enhances satisfaction, helping you feel full and content sooner.</p></div>
<div class="fact-row"><p class="fact-row__name">Caramelized Onion &amp; Goat Cheese Focaccia</p><p>Onions contain prebiotic fibers that feed beneficial gut bacteria. Fermented cheeses like goat cheese are easier to digest and contribute to gut microbiome diversity.</p></div>
</div>

## What's inside your cup?

<div class="fact-grid">
<div class="fact-row"><p class="fact-row__name">Banana Bread Latte</p><p>Bananas naturally contain potassium and vitamin B6, nutrients involved in muscle function and serotonin production.</p></div>
<div class="fact-row"><p class="fact-row__name">Cookie Butter Latte</p><p>Made with spiced biscuit flavors: spices such as cinnamon and nutmeg contain plant compounds (polyphenols) that support metabolic health and reduce inflammation in small daily doses.</p></div>
<div class="fact-row"><p class="fact-row__name">Whole Milk</p><p>Contains protein, fats, and calcium that help slow caffeine absorption and keep you fuller longer. The natural fats also help your body absorb fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) and support sustained energy.</p></div>
<div class="fact-row"><p class="fact-row__name">Oat Milk</p><p>Naturally contains beta-glucans, a soluble fiber known to support heart health and stable blood sugar. It's also gentle on digestion and provides longer-lasting carbohydrate energy.</p></div>
</div>

*We're not trying to make food restrictive or clinical. We just want to remind you that everyday ingredients quietly care for you :)*
